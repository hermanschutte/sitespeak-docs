Each client you create has a fully isolated workspace with their own chatbots, training sources, conversations, and leads. Clients cannot see each other's data, and you can switch between them instantly from your agency dashboard.

<Note>
  Clients do not have their own SiteSpeak login. They are managed entirely through your agency dashboard. You control all access and configuration on their behalf.
</Note>

## Creating a Client

1. Go to **Agency > Clients** in your dashboard.
2. Click **Create Client**.
3. Enter the client's name and confirm.

The new client workspace is created immediately. You can now switch into it to start building chatbots.

## Switching to a Client

To work inside a client's workspace, use the **client switcher** in the top navigation bar. Select the client from the dropdown to switch context - all chatbots, sources, and settings you see will belong to that client.

You can also click **Switch** next to any client in the **Agency > Clients** list to jump directly to their workspace.

To return to your main agency account, select your agency name from the same switcher.

## Building Chatbots for Clients

Once you've switched into a client workspace, creating and managing chatbots works exactly the same as for your own account. All standard features are available - training sources, tools, appearance settings, integrations, and more.

Refer to the main SiteSpeak documentation for chatbot setup:
- [Create Your Chatbot](/getting-started/create-your-chatbot)
- [Training Your Chatbot](/training-your-chatbot/overview)
- [Tools & Actions](/tools-and-actions/overview)

## Plan Limits

Each client counts toward your plan's client limit:

| Plan | Clients |
|------|---------|
| Starter | 10 |
| Pro | 25 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

If you need more clients than your current plan allows, upgrade at [sitespeak.ai/agencies](https://sitespeak.ai/agencies).
