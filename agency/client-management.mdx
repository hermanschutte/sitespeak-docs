Each client you create has a fully isolated workspace with their own chatbots, training sources, conversations, and leads. Clients cannot see each other's data, and you can switch between them instantly from your agency dashboard.

## Creating a Client

1. Go to **Agency > Clients** in your dashboard.
2. Click **Add Client**.
3. Enter the client's name and confirm.

The new client workspace is created immediately. You can now switch into it to start building chatbots.

## Viewing Client Chatbots

Each client row in the **Agency > Clients** table can be expanded to see all of their chatbots at a glance. Click the client name (or the chevron) to expand the row and view their agents with key stats: sources, messages, and leads.

From the expanded view, click **Manage** next to any chatbot to jump directly into that chatbot's dashboard.

## Managing a Client's Chatbot

When you open a client's chatbot, a banner appears at the top of the page showing which client you're managing. This helps you stay oriented when working across multiple clients.

To return to the agency dashboard, use the **Back to Agency** link in the sidebar (below "Install Agent") or in the profile menu.

## Inviting Client Users

You can give your clients direct access to their own dashboard and inbox. When invited, a client user can log in (using your branded login page if you have a custom domain) and access only their workspace - they cannot see other clients or your agency settings.

### How to Invite a Client User

1. Go to **Agency > Clients** in your dashboard
2. Click the actions menu (three dots) next to the client
3. Select **Users**
4. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
5. Choose a role (Administrator, Editor, or Member)
6. Click **Add Team Member**

The invitee receives an email invitation with your agency branding. If you have a custom domain configured, the invitation link will use your branded URL.

<Tip>
  Send invitations while accessing the dashboard through your custom domain. This ensures the invite email and registration flow use your agency branding instead of SiteSpeak's.
</Tip>

### What Client Users Can See

Client users are scoped to their specific client workspace. They can:

- View and manage chatbots belonging to their client account
- Access the inbox and respond to conversations
- View leads and conversation history
- Manage training sources (within their limits)

Client users **cannot** see or access:

- Other client accounts
- The agency dashboard or settings
- Billing or subscription information
- Agency team management

### Client User Roles

The same roles available for agency team members apply to client users:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Administrator** | Full access to client chatbots, settings, and user management |
| **Editor** | Can view and respond to messages in the inbox and update chatbot answers |
| **Member** | Can view and respond to messages in the inbox |

## Per-Client Usage Limits

You can set individual message credit and source limits for each client. This lets you control how much of your agency's total allocation each client can use.

### Setting Limits

In the **Agency > Clients** table, each client row has **Messages** and **Sources** columns. Click on these fields to set a limit:

1. Click the **Set limit** text (or the existing limit value) in the Messages or Sources column
2. Enter the limit number
3. Press **Enter** or click away to save

Leave a field blank to remove the limit. Clients without explicit limits share the agency's total pool.

### How Limits Work

- **Message credits**: The limit controls how many message credits a client can use per month. When a client reaches their limit, their chatbots will stop responding until the next billing cycle or until you increase the limit.
- **Sources**: The limit controls how many training sources a client can add across all their chatbots. When reached, the client cannot add new sources until the limit is increased or existing sources are removed.
- **Agency ceiling**: Per-client limits work alongside your plan's total limits. Even if a client has remaining allocation, they cannot exceed the agency's overall plan limit.

### Over-Allocation Warning

If the sum of all client limits exceeds your agency's plan total, a warning banner appears at the top of the clients table. This is informational only - the agency plan total remains the hard ceiling.

<Note>
  Per-client limits are soft limits. Setting them helps you manage allocation across clients, but your agency plan's total limits are always the hard ceiling regardless of individual client settings.
</Note>

## Client API Keys (BYOK)

You can let a client bring their own LLM API keys, so their chatbots run on the client's own provider accounts. This is off by default and is enabled per client with the `byok_enabled` field on the [Create](/api-reference/agency/create-client) and [Update](/api-reference/agency/update-client) Client Workspace API endpoints.

### What Clients Get

With BYOK enabled, workspace administrators see an **LLM API Keys** page in their profile settings where they can add keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Cohere. They can also set OpenAI and Anthropic keys per chatbot in each chatbot's **AI Model** settings. Keys are validated against the provider before they are saved.

Key priority for a client chatbot: **chatbot key > workspace key > agency account key**. Your own account-level keys keep serving all client workspaces that have no key of their own, whether or not BYOK is enabled for them.

### Credits Still Count

Messages from client chatbots always count against the client's message credits and your agency pool, no matter whose key served them. Per-client limits keep working with BYOK enabled. The provider usage itself is billed by the provider to whoever owns the key.

### Disabling BYOK

Set `byok_enabled` to `false` to switch a client back off. Their key pages disappear, and any stored keys stay saved but stop serving; the client's chatbots fall back to your agency keys or platform keys. Enabling again restores the stored keys.

<Note>
  A key that the provider rejects (revoked, expired, or out of provider credit) does not fall back to other keys. The client's messages on that provider fail until the key is updated or removed. A failing Cohere key normally just skips search result reranking, so answers keep flowing with lower ranking quality.
</Note>

## Creating Chatbots for Clients

You can create chatbots for a client directly from the agency dashboard without leaving the page:

1. Click the actions menu (three dots) next to the client
2. Select **Create Agent**
3. Enter the agent name, an optional website URL, and choose a theme color
4. Click **Create Agent**

If you provide a website URL, the site will be crawled and the chatbot will start training automatically. After creation, you'll be taken to the chatbot's sources page to add more training data.

Once inside a client's chatbot, managing it works exactly the same as your own chatbots. All standard features are available - training sources, tools, appearance settings, integrations, and more.

Refer to the main SiteSpeak documentation for chatbot setup:
- [Create Your Chatbot](/getting-started/create-your-chatbot)
- [Training Your Chatbot](/training-your-chatbot/overview)
- [Tools & Actions](/tools-and-actions/overview)

## Client Actions

From the actions menu (three dots) next to any client in the **Agency > Clients** table:

- **View Agents** - Expand the row to see the client's chatbots
- **Users** - Manage users invited to this client's workspace
- **Create Agent** - Create a new chatbot for this client
- **Rename** - Change the client's display name
- **Delete** - Permanently remove the client and all their chatbots, sources, and data. This action cannot be undone.

## Plan Limits

|------|---------|
| Starter | 10 |
| Pro | 25 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

If you need more clients than your current plan allows, upgrade at [sitespeak.ai/agencies](https://sitespeak.ai/agencies).
