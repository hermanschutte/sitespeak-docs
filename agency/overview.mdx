The SiteSpeak Agency & Reseller Program lets you deliver AI chatbots to your clients under your own brand - no SiteSpeak branding in sight. Upload your logo, add your domain, and manage all your clients from a single dashboard. It's white-label AI chatbot software built for agencies and resellers.

## What's Included

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="White-Label Branding" icon="paintbrush" href="/agency/branding">
    Replace SiteSpeak branding with your own logo and brand name across the dashboard and all client-facing emails.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Client Management" icon="users" href="/agency/client-management">
    Create and manage isolated client workspaces from a single agency dashboard. Each client gets their own chatbots, sources, and conversations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom Domain" icon="globe" href="/agency/custom-domain">
    Serve your agency dashboard on your own domain (e.g. `app.youragency.com`) with automatic HTTPS. Available on Pro and Enterprise plans.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Branded Emails" icon="mail" href="/agency/branding#email-branding">
    All transactional emails (lead captures, escalations, summaries) go out under your agency name, not SiteSpeak's.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
