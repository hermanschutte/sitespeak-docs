Invite colleagues to help manage your agency. When you add a team member at the agency level, they automatically get access to **all client accounts** with the same role - no need to invite them to each client individually.

## How It Works

Agency team membership uses a hierarchy model:

1. You invite a team member to your **agency team** with a specific role
2. That member automatically inherits access to **every client sub-team**
3. Their permissions across all clients match their agency-level role

When you add a new client, existing team members can immediately access it. No extra setup required.

## Inviting Team Members

1. Navigate to **Agency > Team** in your agency dashboard
2. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
3. Select a role (see below)
4. Click **Add Team Member**

The invitee receives an email invitation. If they already have an account, they can accept and start working immediately. If not, they can create an account and accept the invitation.

<Note>
  If you have a custom domain configured, send invitations while on your custom domain so the invite link and registration flow use your agency branding.
</Note>

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Administrator** | Full access to all client chatbots, settings, and team management |
| **Editor** | Can view and respond to messages in the inbox and update chatbot answers |
| **Member** | Can view and respond to messages in the inbox |

All roles apply across every client account in your agency.

## Managing Team Members

From the **Agency > Team** page you can:

- **Change roles** - Click the role name next to a team member to update their permissions
- **Remove members** - Click "Remove" to revoke a team member's access to the agency and all client accounts
- **Cancel invitations** - Cancel pending invitations that haven't been accepted yet

## FAQ

**Can a team member have different roles for different clients?**

The role assigned at the agency level applies to all client accounts. If you need someone to have access to a specific client only, invite them as a [client user](/agency/client-management#inviting-client-users) instead. Client users are scoped to a single client workspace and cannot see other clients or agency settings.

**Do team members count against any limits?**

No. There is no limit on the number of team members you can invite to your agency.

**Will team members see the agency dashboard?**

Yes. Team members can see the agency dashboard, switch between client accounts, and perform actions based on their assigned role.
