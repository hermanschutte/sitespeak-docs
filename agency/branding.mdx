All branding settings are found under **Agency > Branding** in your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately across the dashboard and all outgoing emails.

## Logo & Favicon

Upload your agency logo and favicon from the **Logo** section. Supported formats: PNG, SVG, JPG. Recommended logo size: 200x50px or wider at 2x resolution for sharp rendering on retina displays.

Your logo appears in:
- The dashboard header for you and all your clients
- Transactional emails (lead capture notifications, escalation alerts, conversation summaries)

<Note>
  Your logo appears in all transactional emails sent to your clients, including lead capture confirmations, escalation notifications, and conversation summary emails.
</Note>

## Brand Name

Enter your agency or product name in the **Brand Name** field. This name is used:
- In email subject lines and sender display names
- As a fallback when no logo image is set
- In the browser tab title

## Email Branding

Control how your agency appears in outgoing emails:

**From Name** - The sender name clients see in their inbox (e.g. "Acme Support" instead of "SiteSpeak"). Set this to your agency name or your client-facing product name.

**Sign-off Name** - The name used in the closing line of email bodies (e.g. "The Acme Team"). This appears at the bottom of escalation and notification emails.

## Upgrade URL

By default, upgrade buttons in the dashboard open the SiteSpeak pricing page. Set an **Upgrade URL** to send your client workspace users to your own pricing or billing page instead.

When set, every upgrade button shown to users in a client workspace redirects to your URL, with the user's email address appended as an identifier:

```
https://youragency.com/pricing?email=customer%40example.com
```

If your URL already contains query parameters, the `email` parameter is appended with `&`.

- The redirect only applies to users in client workspaces. Your own agency team keeps the default behavior.
- Leave the field empty to keep the default pricing page for everyone.
- Include the protocol (e.g. `https://youragency.com/pricing`).

## Powered By

By default, the chat widget shows a small "Powered by SiteSpeak" badge. You can customize or remove this from the **Powered By** section in branding settings:

- **Show badge** - Leave the default badge visible
- **Custom text** - Replace it with your own "Powered by [Your Agency]" text
- **Hide** - Remove the badge entirely (available on Pro and Enterprise plans)
