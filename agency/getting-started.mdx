<Accordion title="Video transcript">
Your clients want an AI agent on their website. You can build it for them, and sell it under your own brand.

With a SiteSpeak agency plan, your clients see your logo, your domain, and your emails. This video shows how to set up your brand, add a client, build the agent, and give the client access.

Step 1. Choose an agency plan. Go to sitespeak.ai/agencies, and select an agency plan. Agency Starter costs 399 dollars a month, for 10 clients. That is about 40 dollars for each client. Agency Pro costs 699 dollars a month, for 25 clients. Agency Enterprise costs 1,299 dollars a month, with no limit on clients. Annual plans cost less. Then open the profile menu, and click Agency.

Step 2. Add your brand. Open Branding. Type your Brand Name. Upload your logo and your favicon. Your clients see this brand in their dashboard.

Step 3. Use your own domain. Under Custom Domain, type a domain. For example: app.youragency.com. Add the CNAME record to the DNS of your domain. When the domain is verified, your clients log in at your domain.

Step 4. Brand the emails and the chat widget. Under Email Branding, type the From Name and the Sign-off Name. These settings apply to lead capture notifications, escalation emails, and insight digests. Under Widget Branding, type your Powered By Text and your Powered By URL. Now the chat widget links to your agency. Click Save.

Step 5. Add a client. Open Clients, and click Add Client. Type the Client Name. Then click Create Client. Each client has a separate workspace, with its own agents, sources, messages, and leads.

Step 6. Build the agent for the client. Open the menu of the client, and click Create Agent. Type the agent name and the website URL of the client. Select a Theme Color. Then click Create Agent. SiteSpeak trains the agent on the website of the client. Click Manage to test the agent, and to add more sources. When the agent is ready, install it on the website of the client.

Step 7. Give the client access. Open the menu of the client, and click Users. Type the email address of the client. Then click Add Team Member. The client logs in to your branded dashboard, and can read the conversations in the Inbox. In Branding, under Upgrade URL, add the link to your pricing page. When a client clicks Upgrade, the client goes to your page.

You set the price for your clients. You keep the client relationship.

Start at sitespeak.ai/agencies.
</Accordion>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for an Agency plan">
    Head to [sitespeak.ai/agencies#pricing](https://sitespeak.ai/agencies#pricing) and choose the plan that fits your agency size - Starter (up to 10 clients), Pro (up to 25 clients with custom domain), or Enterprise (unlimited).

    Once your account is created you'll land in your agency dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your branding">
    Go to **Agency > Branding** in the dashboard. Upload your logo and set your brand name.

    This branding is applied to your dashboard and all emails sent to your clients. See the [Branding guide](/agency/branding) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first client account">
    Go to **Agency > Clients** and click **Create Client**. Enter the client's name and confirm.

    A new isolated workspace is created for them with their own chatbots, training sources, and conversation history. See [Client Management](/agency/client-management) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a chatbot for the client">
    Use the client switcher in the top navigation to switch into your new client's workspace. From there, create and configure chatbots exactly as you would for your own account.

    All the standard SiteSpeak features are available - training sources, tools, appearance settings, and more. See [Create Your Chatbot](/getting-started/create-your-chatbot) if you need a refresher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set client limits (optional)">
    Back on the **Agency > Clients** page, you can set per-client message credit and source limits. Click the **Set limit** text in the Messages or Sources column for any client to allocate a specific amount.

    This is useful for distributing your agency's total allocation across clients. See [Per-Client Usage Limits](/agency/client-management#per-client-usage-limits) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite client users (optional)">
    If your client wants direct access to their dashboard and inbox, you can invite them. Click the actions menu next to the client and select **Users**, then invite them by email.

    Client users can only see their own workspace - they cannot access other clients or your agency settings. If you have a custom domain, they will see your branded login page. See [Inviting Client Users](/agency/client-management#inviting-client-users) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members (optional)">
    Go to **Agency > Team** and invite colleagues by email. Choose a role for each member - Administrator, Editor, or Member.

    Team members automatically get access to all client accounts with the same role. No need to invite them to each client individually. See [Team Members](/agency/team-members) for details on roles and permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a custom domain (optional, Pro+)">
    If you're on the Pro or Enterprise plan, you can serve the dashboard on your own domain so clients access it at `app.youragency.com` instead of `app.sitespeak.ai`.

    Follow the [Custom Domain guide](/agency/custom-domain) to configure your DNS and verify your domain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Client workspaces are managed through your agency dashboard. You can invite agency team members to help manage all clients, or invite client-specific users who only see their own workspace.
</Note>
