This guide walks you through everything you need to go from signup to delivering your first white-label AI chatbot to a client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for an Agency plan">
    Head to [sitespeak.ai/agencies#pricing](https://sitespeak.ai/agencies#pricing) and choose the plan that fits your agency size - Starter (up to 10 clients), Pro (up to 25 clients with custom domain), or Enterprise (unlimited).

    Once your account is created you'll land in your agency dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your branding">
    Go to **Agency > Branding** in the dashboard. Upload your logo and set your brand name.

    This branding is applied to your dashboard and all emails sent to your clients. See the [Branding guide](/agency/branding) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first client account">
    Go to **Agency > Clients** and click **Create Client**. Enter the client's name and confirm.

    A new isolated workspace is created for them with their own chatbots, training sources, and conversation history. See [Client Management](/agency/client-management) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a chatbot for the client">
    Use the client switcher in the top navigation to switch into your new client's workspace. From there, create and configure chatbots exactly as you would for your own account.

    All the standard SiteSpeak features are available - training sources, tools, appearance settings, and more. See [Create Your Chatbot](/getting-started/create-your-chatbot) if you need a refresher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a custom domain (optional, Pro+)">
    If you're on the Pro or Enterprise plan, you can serve the dashboard on your own domain so clients access it at `app.youragency.com` instead of `app.sitespeak.ai`.

    Follow the [Custom Domain guide](/agency/custom-domain) to configure your DNS and verify your domain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your clients do not have their own SiteSpeak logins. All client workspaces are managed entirely through your agency dashboard.
</Note>
