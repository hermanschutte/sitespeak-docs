You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Silex** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Silex with SiteSpeakAI.

**Silex** is a platform for hosting and deploying static websites and applications. It offers a simple and streamlined way to get your content online. Silex is a free/libre website builder designed to provide a seamless experience, whether you're a beginner just starting out or an experienced developer looking for advanced customization.

**Website URL:** [Silex](https://silex.me/). The following [documentation](https://docs.silex.me/en/home) aims to guide users and developers through the features and configuration of Silex.

## Add your AI support agent to your **Silex** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Silex

1. Log into your **Silex** account and open the website you want to add your AI support agent to

![Dashboard](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Silex/1-Silex-Dashboard.png)

2. Click on **Settings** on the left side toolbar

![Settings](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Silex/2-Silex-Settings.png)

3. Select the **Code** tab under **Site Settings**, insert your snippet  in the editor text box and click on **Apply**

![Code editor-Insert snippet](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Silex/3-Silex-Insert-snippet.png)

4. Click on **Publish** to publish the changes to your live website

![Publish website](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Silex/4-Silex-Publish-website.png)

5. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Silex** website page

![Agent button displayed](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Silex/5-Silex-agent-button.png)

6. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue

![Agent dialogue](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Silex/6-Silex-agent-dialogue.png)

That's it! You have successfully added your SiteSpeakAI no-code AI support agent to your **Silex** website. Your customers/visitors can now start interacting with it, asking all the questions they have.
