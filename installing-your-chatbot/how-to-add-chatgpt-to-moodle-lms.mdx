You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Moodle** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Moodle with SiteSpeakAI.

**Moodle** (pronounced "mood-ull") is a free and open-source **Online Learning Platform** or **Learning Management System**. Think of it like a virtual classroom. It's used by schools, universities, businesses, and even individuals to create and deliver online courses, training programs, and learning materials. 

It allows instructors to:

*   **Upload materials:** Share documents, videos, presentations, etc.
*   **Create assignments:**  Set quizzes, tests, and projects.
*   **Facilitate discussions:**  Provide forums for students to interact.
*   **Track progress:** Monitor student performance.

It's very customizable and can be adapted to fit a wide range of learning needs.

**Website URL:** [Moodle](https://moodle.org/)

## Add your AI support agent to your **Moodle** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Moodle

1. Log into your **Moodle** admin account and select **Site administration** on the top menu
![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/mb0V8Wp3KKZ4bNzeG0vZjV5uXI5ZW1PboqrU9Z3F.png)

2. Select **Appearance** under **Site administration**
![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/0QGCiCxyZ3oUQzvKmnWNvHQfbNLpUsG6cueOPi6v.png)

3. Select **Additional HTML** under **Appearance**
![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/pBoWQE10oPmZE1tIH5b5WtRLTJEDtttglrejf25c.png)

4. Insert your AI support agent snippet under **Within HEAD**
![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/synIPkV8IDWBcpuRw8tT4JbiepfeRfD9oiEjOgBV.png)

5. Scroll down on the **Additional HTML** page and click on **Save changes**
![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/CrozeEtfM9lxOxPIZqu3kMY9scRr8sHcIJlFMUZn.png)

6. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Moodle** website page
![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/u6TO5Erpp1kpVnMuPqevbFmnVQ8O1yfpf7nDSQhq.png)

7. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue
![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/CITnqkPSHe2p5L4L4iXJedtCaik1tYe8txgKOV67.png)

Your AI support agent has now been added to your **Moodle** website and your visitors/customers can start interacting with it!
