You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Duda** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Duda with SiteSpeakAI.

**Duda** is a website builder designed for entrepreneurs and small businesses. It allows users to create and manage websites without coding, using AI, templates or from scratch.

**Website URL:** [Duda](https://www.duda.co/)

## Add your AI support agent to your **Duda** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Duda

1. Log into your **Duda** account and on your dashboard select your site under **Sites**

![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/duda/1-Select-your-site.png)

**Note!** Duda allows adding your snippet site wide as well as per page. Adding site wide is achieved by editing the **Settings** and per page is achieved by adding the **Html** widget to the page.

#### A. Site Wide

1. Click on the **More** item on the left side toolbar and select **Settings**

![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/duda/2-Select-Settings.png)

2. Select **Head HTML** and insert your AI support agent snippet in the **Body End HTML** input box. Click on **Save** and then on **Publish**

![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/duda/5-Insert-snippet-Update-and-Publish.png)

#### C. For all options

1. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your live **Duda** website page

![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/duda/6-Chatbot-button.png)

2. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue

![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/duda/7-Chatbot-dialogue.png)


Your AI support agent has now been added to your **Duda** website and your visitors/customers can start interacting with it!
