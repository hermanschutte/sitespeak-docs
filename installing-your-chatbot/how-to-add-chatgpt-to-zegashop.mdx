You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Zegashop** website page. Here's how to add ChatGPT to Zegashop with SiteSpeakAI.

**Zegashop** is an all-in-one, AI-powered e-commerce platform and website builder. It lets users create online stores, manage product listings, process orders, and run retail businesses without needing coding skills

**Website URL**: [Zegashop](https://www.zegashop.com/)

## Add your AI support agent to your **Zegashop** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Zegashop

1. Log into your **Zegashop** account and on the dashboard, select **Configure**
![1 - Zegashop - Select Configure on dashboard.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/FoFDju4Vz7tYgZBN5yqCYdY7LKjLIZalhUgztHXj.png)

2. Select **General** under Configure
![2 - Zegashop - Select General under Configure.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/gfyfZ6JFWXDQCzvUcER7mx4Uhiggti6sfbJm8kKr.png)

3. Select the **Content** tab, insert your AI support agent snippet in the **Custom Javascript** Scripts box and click on **Save**
![3 - Zegashop - Insert your snippet and click on Save.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/nT21mjsdphpiCU7IehoF03RaBsh03UpE3IGCgIOP.png)

4. Voila! Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Zegashop** website
![4 - Zegashop - Button on your webpage](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/8hFaFgU8KPqW7fwSY0n3bHJAGW7o2iYK7eW01f4P.png)

5. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue
![5 - Zegashop - Chatbot dialogue on your webpage](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/HCCW4MmY4DQqJs4GU87WTpVQwaXU8OccPfXIGka4.png)

Your AI support agent is now added to your **Zegashop** website and your customers/visitors can start interacting with it!
