You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Photler** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Photler with SiteSpeakAI.

**Photler** is a tool that drives travel inspiration. If you wish to show others the magical places you’ve been to or see scenic places that you should visit, Photler is the place to go to. It brings together travel enthusiasts from all over the world and aims at making your travel decisions easier by showing you how beautiful the world actually is.

**Website URL:** [Photler](https://photler.com/)

## Add your AI support agent to your **Photler** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Photler

1. Log into your **Photler** website and on your dashboard, select **SETTINGS** on the left sidebar.

![1 - Photler - Select SETTINGS on your dashboard.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/fKcaTvLkudqfA5ZK5QPUjC12kWJD60ojgMB6dBY6.png)

2. Select **Site settings**.

![2 - Photler - Select Site settings.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/oJYdH9DkSMUN6uwTnjDYtkgm2QdccWzK2YGkmikR.png)

3. Scroll down on the SITE SETTINGS list and insert your snippet under **Custom code**. Click on **SAVE** when done.

![3 - Photler - Insert your snippet.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/1FVBZT4Com6392lcICTyQ30iUfMQdmyDLecILDU8.png)

4. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Photler** website.

![4 - Photler - AI support agent button.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/bNV0zz0CMtLhBwW79VBPAqJPpVj1zgGUH8W3uKnf.png)

5. If you click on the button it will display your AI support agent dialogue.

![5 - Photler - AI support agent dialogue.png](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/nOJJ9Rg6c2lNe9Pz8tbvQZI8ZGxGdswNFQJGoH9z.png)

Your AI support agent is now added to your **Photler** website and your customers/visitors can start interacting with it!
