You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Unbounce** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Unbounce with SiteSpeakAI.

**Unbounce** is a conversion optimization platform used to create and manage landing pages. With Unbounce's no-code, AI-powered tools at your fingertips, you can launch, A/B test, and optimize landing pages on your own, in a single platform.

**Website URL**: [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com)

## Add your AI support agent to your **Unbounce** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Unbounce

1. Log into your **Unbounce** account and on your dashboard, select the page you want to add your AI support agent to
![1-Unbounce-Dashboard-select-page](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/1-Unbounce-Dashboard-select-page.png)

2. On the next screen, select **Edit** to start editing your page
![2-Unbounce-Select-Edit](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/2-Unbounce-Select-Edit.png)

3. Drag the **Custom HTML** item onto your page (you can place it anywhere, it won't display above other sections, it will display at the right hand bottom of the page)
![3-Unbounce-Drag-Custom-HTML](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/3-Unbounce-Drag-Custom-HTML.png)

4. Double-click on the Custom HTML placeholder, insert your snippet code in the **Embed Custom HTML Code** box and click on **Save Code**
![4-Unbounce-Insert-snippet-in-Embed-Custom-HTML](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/4-Unbounce-Insert-snippet-in-Embed-Custom-HTML.png)

5. Click on **Save** to save your changes to your live page
![5-Unbounce-Click-on-Save](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/5-Unbounce-Click-on-Save.png)

6. Click on **Publish** to publish your page
![6-Unbounce-Click-on-Publish](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/6-Unbounce-Click-on-Publish.png)

7. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Unbounce** website page
![7-Unbounce-Agent-Button](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/7-Unbounce-Agent-Button.png)

8. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue
![8-Unbounce-Agent-Dialogue](/images/installing-your-chatbot/Unbounce/8-Unbounce-Agent-Dialogue.png)

Your AI support agent has now been added to your **Unbounce** website and your visitors/customers can start interacting with it!
