You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Droip** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Droip with SiteSpeakAI.

**Droip** is a no-code website builder focused on speed and simplicity. It's particularly known for its incredibly fast page building – claiming to build a fully functional website in just 60 seconds. It's designed for entrepreneurs and small businesses who want a quick and easy way to launch a website without needing coding skills. It offers a limited selection of templates and customization options, prioritizing speed over extensive design flexibility.

**Website:** [Droip](https://droip.com/)

## Add your AI support agent to your **Droip** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Droip

1. Log into your **Droip** account and select **Pages** on the top menu
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/1-Droip-Select-Pages.png)

2. Select the page you want to add your agent to
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/2-Droip-Select-your-page.png)

3. Select **Elements** on the top menu bar, scroll down to **Components** and drag the **Code** element onto the Header on your page
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/3-Droip-Drag-Code-element-onto-Header.png)

4. Select **More Otions** for the **Code** element
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/4-Droip-Select-More-Options-for-the-Code-element.png)

5. This will display the **Custom Code Editor** allowing you to add your agent snippet
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/5-Droip-This-will-display-the-Custom-Code-Editor.png)

6. Insert your code snippet and click on **Embed**
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/6-Droip-Insert-your-Code-and-click-on-Embed.png)

7. Click on **Preview** to preview your inserted agent button
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/7-Droip-Click-on-Preview.png)

8. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Droip** website page
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/8-Droip-Chatbot-button-on-Droip-page.png)

9. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/droip/9-Droip-Chatbot-dialogue-on-Droip-page.png)

Your AI support agent has now been added to your **Droip** website and your visitors/customers can start interacting with it!
