You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Durable** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Durable with SiteSpeakAI.

**Durable** is a website builder focused on creating beautiful, accessible, and performant websites quickly – particularly for businesses and nonprofits. It emphasizes a "no-code" approach, letting you build visually with drag-and-drop elements, but also offers powerful backend features like content relationships and data management, making it ideal for complex sites. It's known for its speed, SEO optimization, and commitment to accessibility standards.

**Website URL:** [Durable](https://www.durable.io/)

## Add your AI support agent to your **Durable** website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Durable

1. Log in to your **Durable** website builder dashboard and select **Website**
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/durable/1-Durable-Select-Website-on-dashboard.png)

2. Select the **Settings** tab
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/durable/2-Durable-Select-the-Settings-tab.png)

3. Insert your support agent snippet in the **Head Code** text input box under **Custom code** and click on **Save** to save the changes to your website
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/durable/3-Durable-Insert-snippet-and-Save.png)

4. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Durable** website page
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/durable/4-Durable-chatbot-button.png)

5. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue
![](/images/installing-your-chatbot/durable/5-Durable-chatbot-dialogue.png)

Your AI support agent has now been added to your **Durable** website and your visitors/customers can start interacting with it!
