You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **webstarts** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to webstarts with SiteSpeakAI.

**webstarts** is a web hosting and website builder platform. It provides tools for creating and hosting websites without requiring coding knowledge.

**Website URL**: [webstarts](https://www.webstarts.com/)

## Add your AI support agent to your webstarts website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In webstarts

1. Log into your **webstarts** account and on the dashboard, select the site that you want to add your support agent snippet to and click on **Edit Site**

![1](/images/installing-your-chatbot/webstarts/1-dashboard.png)

2. On the Editor page, click on **Add** in the leftside toolbar to add a new element to your website

![2](/images/installing-your-chatbot/webstarts/2-add-element.png)

3. Scroll down on the list of elements and select **Embed Code**

![3](/images/installing-your-chatbot/webstarts/3-select-embed-code.png)

When adding your snippet you have 2 choices as where to insert it, i.e. site wide (View Site Head) or per page (View Page Head)

4. Insert your snippet in the **Edit HTML** edit text area and click on **Insert**

![4](/images/installing-your-chatbot/webstarts/4-insert-your-snippet.png)

5. Click on **View Site** to view your live website

![5](/images/installing-your-chatbot/webstarts/5-click-on-view-site.png)

6. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **webstarts** website page

![6](/images/installing-your-chatbot/webstarts/6-chatbot-button.png)

7. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue

![7](/images/installing-your-chatbot/webstarts/7-chatbot-dialogue.png)

Your AI support agent has now been added to your **webstarts** website and your visitors/customers can start interacting with it!
