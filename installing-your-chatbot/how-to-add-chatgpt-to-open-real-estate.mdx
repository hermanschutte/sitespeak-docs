You want to embed your custom trained AI support agent in your **Open Real Estate** website page. Here's how to add an AI support agent to Open Real Estate with SiteSpeakAI.

**Open Real Estate** lets you create your own website to get the profits within the shortest possible time or to present your real estate agency on the Internet. **Website URL:** [Open Real Estate](https://open-real-estate.info/)

## Add your AI support agent to your Open Real Estate website

<InstallCopyCode />

### In Open Real Estate

1. Log into your **Open Real Estate** account and on your dashboard, select **Info Pages** under **Content**

![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/hDxKYijsOqZibkQmzn47HgMM1QqVkpp4J7ndeuUP.png)

2. Click on **Update** for the page you want to add your AI support agent snippet to

![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/AUDZy0y7f5AIzXcigSNjpS3xRwjnQCoOD2YpgAwG.png)

3. Select **Source** and insert your AI support agent snippet after any content that already exists on the page

![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/1HgcZ4dWL9HZbLoOB2tcL8eY3915x6HWIfjZTFjz.png)

4. Scroll down on the page editor and click on **Save** or **Save and close**

![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/K1dHE3MNBtv6kcZ436PLSfmV96Vrn7Ho2heodaWi.png)

5. Your AI support agent button will now be displayed on your **Open Real Estate** website page

![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/sOv7xmsDYit8mw6pKZHXsw7N4pEjyyblFAYJVvBT.png)

6. If you click on the button it will display the AI support agent dialogue

![](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/cdn.sitespeak.ai/uploads/help-center/images/ZbAXmGhYMjUakyr5nlwZL74usf9N5eSJFJlMLlHr.png)

Your AI support agent has now been added to your **Open Real Estate CMS** website and your visitors/customers can start interacting with it!
