The HubSpot Meetings action allows your AI agent to check availability on one of your HubSpot meeting links and help visitors book a meeting directly through the chatbot. Each booking creates or updates the contact in HubSpot and adds the meeting to the organizer's calendar.

## Adding the HubSpot Meetings Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Tools & Actions">
    In your chatbot dashboard, go to **Configuration > Tools & Actions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select HubSpot">
    Find **HubSpot: Make Bookings** and click **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Configuration

### Name

A descriptive name for the action. Default: `book_hubspot_meeting`

### Description

A description of the action and when it should be used. The default description guides the agent on how to handle booking requests, date ranges, and displaying available slots.

### HubSpot Account

Click **Connect HubSpot Account** to connect your HubSpot account via OAuth. You will be redirected to HubSpot to authorize access.

Once connected, your meeting link will be automatically selected. If you have more than one meeting link, you will be prompted to choose which one visitors can book.

<Info>
  This uses the same HubSpot connection as the [HubSpot lead sync integration](/integrations/hubspot). Connecting here also enables lead sync for this chatbot, and disconnecting HubSpot on the Settings page also stops bookings.
</Info>

<Note>
  You need at least one meeting link in HubSpot under **Sales > Meetings**. Meeting links are available on every HubSpot plan, including the free tier.
</Note>

---

## How Bookings Work

1. Visitor asks to book a meeting
2. Agent shows available time slots in a calendar
3. Visitor selects a date and time
4. Visitor fills in their name and email
5. The meeting is booked on your HubSpot meeting link and added to your calendar

Meeting links with more than one duration use the first duration listed on the link. Custom questions on the meeting link are not asked in the chat.

---

## Save the Action

Once you've connected your HubSpot account and selected a meeting link, click **Add Action** to enable it.

Your chatbot will now be able to:
- Check availability on your HubSpot meeting link
- Show available time slots to visitors
- Book meetings for visitors

Bookings appear in HubSpot as meetings on the contact's timeline.
