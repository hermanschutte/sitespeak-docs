SiteSpeakAI integrates with Drip to automatically create and update subscribers in your Drip account when leads are captured by your chatbot. Each lead is also tagged and recorded as a "Lead captured" event, so you can trigger Drip workflows automatically.

## Connect Drip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    In your chatbot dashboard, go to **Configuration > Settings** and click the **Integrations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Drip">
    Locate the **Drip** integration card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click the **Connect** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    A popup will appear asking you to sign in to your Drip account and authorize SiteSpeakAI to connect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connected State

Once connected, you'll see:
- A green **Connected** badge
- Your Drip **Account** name

All newly captured leads will now automatically be added to your Drip account as subscribers.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/integrations/drip-connected.png" alt="Drip connected state" />
</Frame>

## Set a Tag

Every lead synced to Drip can be tagged, so you can segment your subscribers and trigger automations:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Tag field">
    In the connected Drip card, locate the **Tag** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a tag">
    Enter the tag to apply to each subscriber (for example, `SiteSpeak Lead`). Leave it empty to apply no tag.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each captured lead is also recorded in Drip as a **"Lead captured"** event, which you can use as a trigger to start a Drip workflow or campaign.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/integrations/drip-tag.png" alt="Drip tag field" />
</Frame>

## Disconnect Drip

To disconnect the integration, click the **Disconnect** button in the Drip card. You can also use the main toggle at the bottom of the card to temporarily disable syncing without disconnecting.
