Sometimes your visitors need to speak with a human. SiteSpeakAI makes it easy to escalate conversations to your support team when the AI can't fully help.

## How human handoff works

When enabled, visitors can request to speak with a human agent. They'll be prompted to enter their email address, and your team receives a notification with the conversation details.

## Enable escalations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Configuration">
    In your chatbot dashboard, click **Configuration** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Settings">
    Click on **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Escalations tab">
    Click on the **Escalations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Escalations">
    Toggle on **Enable Escalations** to allow visitors to escalate to human support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

## Email notifications

Get notified via email when a visitor escalates:

1. Toggle on **Enable Email Notifications**
2. Enter your email address(es) in the **Escalation Notification Email(s)** field
3. Separate multiple emails with semicolons (`;`)

### Round robin notifications

If you have multiple team members handling support, enable **Round Robin Notifications** to rotate escalations between email addresses instead of sending to all. Each escalation goes to the next email in the list.

<Tip>
  Round robin is great for distributing workload evenly across your support team without overwhelming everyone with notifications.
</Tip>

## Slack notifications

Receive escalation notifications directly in your Slack workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Go to **Install Agent → Slack** and click **Connect to Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Authorize SiteSpeakAI to access your Slack workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite to a channel">
    Invite the SiteSpeakAI bot to the channel where you want to receive notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Slack notifications">
    Back in **Settings → Escalations**, toggle on **Enable Slack Notifications**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You must connect Slack in Deploy & Install before the Slack notification option becomes available.
</Note>

## Webhook notifications

Send escalation data to any URL via a POST request. This is useful for integrating with tools like Zapier, Make, or your own CRM.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable webhook notifications">
    In **Settings → Escalations**, toggle on **Enable Webhook Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your webhook URL">
    Paste the URL where you want to receive escalation data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a signing secret (optional)">
    Enter a secret to sign webhook payloads with HMAC-SHA256 for verification. The signature is sent in the `X-Signature-256` header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Webhook payload

When a visitor escalates, SiteSpeakAI sends a POST request with a JSON body:

```json
{
  "event": "escalation.created",
  "title": "My Chatbot: Visitor needs human assistance",
  "text": "I need to speak with someone",
  "chatbot": {
    "id": "chatbot-uuid",
    "name": "My Chatbot"
  },
  "visitor": {
    "id": 123,
    "email": "visitor@example.com",
    "first_name": "Jane",
    "last_name": "Doe",
    "phone": "+1234567890"
  },
  "conversation": {
    "id": 456,
    "last_message": "I need to speak with someone"
  },
  "inbox_url": "https://app.sitespeak.ai/chatbots/{id}/inbox/{visitor_id}",
  "timestamp": "2026-02-09T12:00:00+00:00"
}
```

### Verifying webhook signatures

If you set a signing secret, each request includes an `X-Signature-256` header. To verify the request is from SiteSpeakAI, compute the HMAC-SHA256 of the raw request body using your secret and compare it to the header value:

```js
const crypto = require("crypto")

function verifySignature(payload, secret, signature) {
  const expected =
    "sha256=" + crypto.createHmac("sha256", secret).update(payload).digest("hex")
  return crypto.timingSafeEqual(
    Buffer.from(expected),
    Buffer.from(signature)
  )
}
```

<Tip>
  Webhook notifications work independently of email and Slack notifications. You can enable any combination of the three.
</Tip>

## How visitors escalate

Once enabled, visitors can escalate to human support by:

- **Asking to speak with a human**: The AI recognizes requests like "Can I talk to a person?" or "I need human help"
- **The AI offering escalation**: When the AI can't confidently answer, it can offer to connect the visitor with a human
- **A rule in your Instructions**: If your [Instructions](/managing-your-chatbot/settings#instructions) say that certain situations should go to your team (for example bugs, outages, login problems, complaints or refund requests), the AI escalates those messages without waiting for the visitor to ask for a person

When a visitor escalates, they'll be prompted to enter their email address so your team can follow up.

<Note>
  Instruction-driven escalation only works while **Enable Escalations** is on. With it off, the AI answers those messages as normal and never opens the escalation form on its own.
</Note>

## Responding via Live Chat

When you receive an escalation notification, you can respond to visitors in real-time through the Inbox (Conversation History).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inbox">
    Click **Inbox** in the dashboard sidebar to see all conversations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the conversation">
    Find the escalated conversation and click on it to open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Live Chat">
    Type your message in the input field and send. This automatically starts a Live Chat session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End Live Chat">
    When finished, click the **End Live Chat** button in the conversation header. This returns control to the AI assistant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/managing-your-chatbot/inbox-live-chat.png" alt="Live Chat in Inbox" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  While Live Chat is active, the AI assistant will not respond to messages. This ensures the visitor only communicates with your support team until you end the session.
</Tip>

## Email replies to visitors

Visitors often leave the chat before your team replies. With email replies on, your Inbox reply also goes to the visitor's email address, and their answer comes back to the same conversation in your Inbox.

<Note>
  Email replies are available on the Pro plan and higher.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    In your chatbot dashboard, click **Configuration**, then **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on email replies">
    On the **Basic** tab, toggle on **Email Replies To Visitors** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How it works

1. You reply to a visitor in the Inbox.
2. If the visitor does not answer in the chat within 5 minutes, SiteSpeakAI emails your reply to them. If you send more than one message, they go out together in one email.
3. The visitor answers the email. Their answer shows in the same conversation in your Inbox, with a mail icon.
4. You reply in the Inbox as usual. Later emails stay in the same email thread.

The email shows your chatbot's name and your first name. It does not show SiteSpeakAI branding. If you are an agency with white-label branding, the email shows your agency logo, and it comes from your verified sending address if you set one.

### When a reply is not emailed

A mail icon next to the send button in the Inbox shows if your reply can go by email. Hover over it to see why when it cannot. A reply is not emailed when:

- The visitor has no email address, or the address is not valid
- An earlier email to the visitor bounced or was marked as spam
- The conversation came from WhatsApp or Google Chat, where the visitor gets your reply in that app

If an email bounces or is marked as spam, the Inbox shows a note under the reply box. Click **Email again** to turn email replies back on for that visitor.

<Tip>
  SiteSpeakAI removes the quoted history from the visitor's email answer, so you only see their new text. Attachments are not shown. The Inbox notes how many the visitor sent.
</Tip>

## Support inbox

With the support inbox on, emails to your support address (for example `support@yourcompany.com`) open conversations in your Inbox. SiteSpeakAI writes a draft answer from your knowledge base. You check the draft, change it if necessary, and send it. The visitor gets your answer by email.

<Note>
  The support inbox is available on the Pro plan and higher. It needs **Email Replies To Visitors** to be on.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the support inbox">
    Go to **Configuration**, then **Settings**. On the **Basic** tab, toggle on **Support Inbox** and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your inbox address">
    After you save, Settings shows an address like `yourcompany-k3f9x2@reply.sitespeak.ai`. Click **Copy address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forward your support email">
    In your email provider, forward your support address to the inbox address. See the steps for your provider below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Forwarding from Gmail or Google Workspace

1. In Gmail, open **Settings**, then **See all settings**, then **Forwarding and POP/IMAP**.
2. Click **Add a forwarding address** and paste your inbox address.
3. Gmail sends a confirmation email to the inbox address. It shows as a new conversation in your SiteSpeakAI Inbox. Open it to find the confirmation code or link.
4. Back in Gmail, enter the code, then select **Forward a copy of incoming mail to** and save.

### Forwarding from Microsoft 365 or Outlook

1. In Outlook, open **Settings**, then **Mail**, then **Forwarding**.
2. Turn on **Enable forwarding** and paste your inbox address.
3. Select **Keep a copy of forwarded messages** if you want the emails in your mailbox too, and save.

<Note>
  Some Microsoft 365 organizations block automatic forwarding to outside addresses. If emails do not arrive, ask your Microsoft 365 admin to allow forwarding for your support mailbox.
</Note>

### How the support inbox works

- Each new email subject gets its own conversation in your Inbox. A reply, or a new email from the same address with the same subject, goes into the same conversation.
- SiteSpeakAI writes a draft answer from your knowledge base and puts it in the reply box, marked as an AI draft. Nothing is sent until you click send.
- Your reply goes to the visitor by email at once, in the same email thread.
- Each AI draft uses one query credit from your monthly [MCP query allowance](/advanced/mcp-endpoint#rate-limits-and-query-credits). When the allowance is used up, emails still arrive, without a draft.

### Sending from your own address

By default, answers come from your chatbot's name at `reply.sitespeak.ai`. To send answers from your own support address, contact us. We verify your domain and set it up for you.

### Limits

- Automatic emails, such as out-of-office replies, do not open a conversation.
- A chatbot receives at most 200 emails a day on the support inbox.
- Attachments are not shown. The Inbox notes how many the sender attached.

## Customizing escalation messages

You can customize the messages shown to visitors during escalation in the **Localization** tab under Settings, including:

- The message shown when escalating
- Email input placeholder text
- Confirmation messages

## Best practices

- **Set expectations**: Let visitors know typical response times in your escalation confirmation message
- **Use round robin**: Distribute escalations evenly across your team
- **Connect Slack**: Get real-time notifications where your team already communicates
- **Use webhooks**: Connect escalations to your CRM or ticketing system via Zapier, Make, or a custom endpoint
- **Review escalations**: Regularly review why visitors escalate to improve your AI's training
